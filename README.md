# I3E Assignment 1
Assignment 1 for Interactive 3D Experience

Key controls
W – Forward
A – Left 
S – Back
D – Right
E – Interact

Instructions
Players are to utilize key controls and navigate through the environment. Players will need to take note of Hazards such as
-	Falling from high ground in the environment where staircases have missing steps, players will lose all their health upon hitting the ground.
-	Flooded Office, contact with the electrified water will result in continuous damage -2 Health per second, will stop when player is not in contact with the electrified water
-	Chemical Spills which result in – 5 Health
-	Laser – 10 Health
Players are to find all collectibles (Some collectibles are hidden in wooden crates)
Players are also required to use the E key to open doors, collect collectibles and destroy wooden crates.
Some of the doors are locked due to the player not hitting a specific requirement such as not acquiring a certain collectible.

Win Condition
Players must collect all collectibles found in the environment and head towards the exit, the player’s health must not be 0 or less otherwise they will lose and not be able to continue playing.

Game Hack
Players can find shortcuts which let them complete getting all the collectibles without risking themselves to danger such as the door found in the environment with missing stairs (factory)

Answer Key
-	To open the first door (nearest to the player when the game is deployed), the player must acquire the flashlight which can be seen at the top of 3 wooden crates.
-	To open the second door, players must acquire a key which can be found in the second floor of the environment with missing stairs (factory) or the environment with spilled barrels of chemical (laboratory).
-	Two Thumb drives are hidden inside wooden crates and can be found in the wooden crate located at the stairs towards the flooded office as well as the wooden crate in the laboratory found from the left side of the entrance.
-	Other collectibles found in the flooded office (laptop and document), in the factory (key and document), in the laboratory (key).

Platform, Hardware & Desired Settings to run the Application
-	Platform: Unity
-	Device: Laptop (Windows)
-	Desired Settings: Free Aspect, Scale 1.5x

Limitations and Bugs
-	Wooden crate might take some time before being destroyed
-	Doors might malfunction
-	Electrified water might not cause any damage
-	Audio for collecting collectibles might be different due to collectibles being close to wooden crates
